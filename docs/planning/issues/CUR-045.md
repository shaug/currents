## Why

Implement pairing-specific256-bit ingress key and epoch, owner-verified one-time QR/system file handoff, SQLCipher evidence-only bundle and encrypted ack.

## Scope

Define pairing-specific 256-bit ingress key/epoch, owner-verified one-time QR and system-file-handoff contract, SQLCipher evidence-only bundle/ack schema and Mac acceptance/ack implementation. Mac deduplicates batch ID/digest, tracks previous digest/gaps and commits receipts before acknowledgment. Prove queue/pairing/rotation/revocation behavior with a synthetic transport and stubbed phone codec; this ticket does not require real iOS encrypted execution. No dataset/recovery key or decisions in ingress. CUR-058 separately implements and qualifies the actual iOS cipher/ABI codec; CUR-046 integrates device protected queue/UI. Capture metadata survives re-pairing and loss gaps remain visible.
