## Why

Create append-only namespaced authoritative tables, exact commit/item order, record/evidence digests, source blobs and encrypted staging.

## Scope

Create append-only namespaced authoritative tables, exact commit/item order, record/evidence digests, source blobs and encrypted staging. Preserve every receipt even when bytes deduplicate. Publish receipt, observations and cursor in one transaction. No deletion/update of committed source/decision content through engine SQL.
