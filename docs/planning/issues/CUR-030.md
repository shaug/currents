## Why

## Scope

Implement Currents-owned encrypted derived-state snapshots, prefix/schema/projector hashes and full read basis. Native ledger remains full replay; no assumed native snapshot API. Trigger after10000 records or clean-close1000 tail; monthly while unlocked compare full replay to snapshot+tail. Restore/upgrade always full replay.
