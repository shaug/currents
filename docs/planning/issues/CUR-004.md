## Why

Create minimal Swift 6 strict-concurrency build fixture and C-header roundtrip, targeting macOS 15.0 arm64 and iOS 18.0 capture proposal.

## Scope

Create minimal Swift 6 strict-concurrency build fixture and C-header roundtrip, targeting macOS 15.0 arm64 and iOS 18.0 capture proposal. Inspect installed full Xcode without assuming CLT selection proves absence; record exact Xcode/SDK/runner build. Test target availability on minimum/current OS. Intel Mac is explicitly unsupported until its own CI/signing qualification. Establish named Xcode schemes CurrentsApp, CurrentsOwner, CurrentsCapture, CurrentsPlatformTests and test plans CurrentsSynthetic/CurrentsProductionQualification. Native xtask runners invoke xcodebuild test -project native/Currents.xcodeproj -scheme CurrentsPlatformTests -testPlan CurrentsSynthetic -destination platform=macOS, arch=arm64 for ordinary synthetic suites; iOS uses CurrentsCapture with exact qualified simulator/device identifier from tools/versions.lock.json. Production qualification uses the signed installed targets and CurrentsProductionQualification, not a synthetic scheme.
