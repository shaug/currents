## Why

Implement bounded analytical cost attribution: FIFO by acquisition effective date/stable ID or previewed selected acquisitions, including unknown/undated opening cohorts.

## Scope

Implement bounded analytical cost attribution: FIFO by acquisition effective date/stable ID or previewed selected acquisitions, including unknown/undated opening cohorts. Quantize native cash carrying amount to cents half-even with recorded residual; final disposal consumes exact remainder. Capitalize buy fees; gross sale gain minus separate fee yields net once. Transfers/splits preserve lineage and corrections close dependent disposals atomically or invalidate current gain.
