## Why

Implement daily1/3/12-month runs and saved immutable inputs/outputs with per-account min/date, coverage, omissions and conflicts.

## Scope

Implement daily1/3/12-month runs and saved immutable inputs/outputs with per-account min/date, coverage, omissions and conflicts. Coherent household base/downside/upside follows design9 external flow/date rules; pure transfers retain shared nominal amount/schedule except explicit override. Card purchase/payment and loan effects separate. Market/FX fixed at basis unless named assumption. Comparison uses original saved output and new actual basis.
