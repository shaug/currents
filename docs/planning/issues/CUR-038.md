## Why

Implement split/merchant/rule correction, scoped reconciliation with exceptions, holdings/basis/valuation inspection, commitment/scenario/forecast comparison and backup/restore/migration workflows.

## Scope

Implement split/merchant/rule correction, scoped reconciliation with exceptions, holdings/basis/valuation inspection, commitment/scenario/forecast comparison and backup/restore/migration workflows. Every durable change uses engine preview/result; Undo compensating decision. Keep warnings, missing basis, partial gain, coverage and scenario meaning adjacent to values.
